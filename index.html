About Me

Hi, I'm Abdullah Ibrahim Elsayed Mohamed Tawfik.

A Systems Analyst bridging business and technology, one diagram at a time.

I have a passion for dissecting complex business processes and rebuilding them into clean, scalable, and compliant system designs. My work sits at the intersection of stakeholder communication, process modeling, and technical documentation.

I don't just draw diagrams. I analyze the "why" behind every decision, define measurable business objectives, and produce artifacts that engineering teams can actually build from — BRDs, SRSs, BPMN workflows, API specs, and traceability matrices.
